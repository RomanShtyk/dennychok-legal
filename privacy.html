Dennychok Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the Dennychok baby tracker app ("the App"), developed by Roman Shtykalo ("I", "me"), handles your information. I am the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data you enter

The App stores what you log about your baby: name, date of birth, sex, feeds, sleep, diapers, growth measurements, temperature, medicines, vaccinations, notes, reminders and photos. This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) so that it syncs between your devices and with caregivers you invite. It is linked to your account and visible only to you and the caregivers you share the baby with. I do not sell it, show ads with it, or use it for anything other than providing the App.

2. Entries about yourself ("For mom")

The App has an optional section for the parent's own wellbeing: glasses of water, a daily mood mark with a note, and your own medication (name, dose, time and the gap between doses that you set). These entries are health data. They are stored in Google Firebase under your account only. Caregivers you invite cannot see them, and they are not part of anything you share. I do not use them for ads, analytics or any purpose other than showing them back to you. The App gives no diagnosis or medical advice; reminders about medication use only the numbers you enter. You can delete single entries in the App, and deleting your account removes all of them.

3. Account (Firebase Authentication)

When you first open the App a guest account is created for you. You can later sign in with Google or Apple to keep your data across devices. Firebase Authentication processes your user ID, sign-in provider, email address and display name, and your IP address for security. Other caregivers of the same baby can see your display name and photo.

4. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. A crash report contains a stack trace, app state, device and app information (model, OS version, app version, free memory) and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports never include the content you enter. They are retained for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store)

Dennychok Premium is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which process your payment; I never see your card details. To know whether Premium is active, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives your App user ID and your purchase history for the App (product, dates, store). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

6. Weather and location

If you use the Weather tab, the App sends the approximate coordinates of your location, or of the city you pick, to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. The request carries no account or device identifier. The chosen place is saved on your device. You can use the rest of the App without granting location access.

7. App integrity (Firebase App Check)

To keep automated abuse away from the database, the App proves that it is a genuine copy using Firebase App Check with Play Integrity (Android) or App Attest (iOS). This involves device and app attestation data processed by Google or Apple; it contains nothing you enter.

8. What I do not collect

The App has no ads and no analytics. It does not track you across other apps or sites. Notifications are scheduled on your device and are not sent through my servers.

9. Sharing with caregivers

When you create an invite code, anyone who enters it in the App gets access to that baby's data at the role you chose (editor or view-only). You can remove caregivers at any time. Each caregiver is responsible for what they do with the data they can see. Entries about yourself (section 2) are never shared this way.

10. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract — storing and syncing your data to provide the App.
  • Contract — checking your Premium purchase.
  • Legitimate interest — crash reporting and keeping the App secure.
  • Consent — signing in with Google or Apple, and sharing with caregivers you invite.

Health-related entries, about your baby (temperature, medicines, vaccinations, growth) or about yourself (mood, medication), are stored only because you choose to enter them, which is your explicit consent to their processing (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). You can leave them out and still use the App, and you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the entries or your account.

11. International transfers

Google (Firebase) and RevenueCat may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

12. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it. In the App you can delete individual entries, a whole baby, or your account (Account → Delete account). Deleting your account removes your account and every baby for which you are the only caregiver, including photos. Babies you share with others stay with the remaining caregivers. Backups and CSV files you export are files on your device and are under your control.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Most of these you can exercise directly in the App (edit or delete entries, export a backup or a CSV file, delete your account). For anything else, contact me at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

14. Children

The App is for parents and caregivers and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). It stores information about your child that you, as their parent or guardian, choose to enter.

15. Changes

I may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
